# D3D promo reel

`d3d_promo.mp4` is a 22-second vertical promo for Instagram Reels and TikTok. It's 1080×1920 at 30 fps, encoded as H.264/AAC, with the audio mastered to −14 LUFS. The style is clean and minimal, built on D3D's yellow accent.

```bash
python3 promo/d3d/make_d3d_promo.py            # final render
python3 promo/d3d/make_d3d_promo.py --preview  # 540×960 draft
```

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0–2.4 s | Hook: "Votre logo. / En porte-clés 3D." The text rises out of a mask and a yellow underline wipes in. |
| 2.4–12 s | Seven real product photos on stacked cards with a slow Ken Burns move. The captions are "Conçu & imprimé en 3D", then "Pour les entreprises", "Pour les marques" and "Pour les événements". |
| 12–16.8 s | "Pourquoi D3D": 100% sur-mesure, maquette 3D gratuite, cadeau d'entreprise idéal. |
| 16.8–22 s | End card: D3D wordmark, "Porte-clés personnalisés", +216 25 430 387, @d3d.tn, and a "Maquette 3D offerte" badge. |

The music is original and generated by the script: 100 BPM soft keys, an arpeggio and light drums, with a swish synced to each card transition.

To change the content, edit `PHOTOS`, `CAPTIONS` and `PROPS` and the end-card strings in `assets()`, then put new photos in `photos/` as 3:4 JPGs.
The "D3D" wordmark is typeset in Inter Display Black. Replace it with the official logo PNG when you have it.
